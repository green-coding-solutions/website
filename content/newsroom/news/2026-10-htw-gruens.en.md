HTW Berlin's Campus Stories features the GRÜNS project ("Gemeinsame Richtlinien zur Überprüfung der ökologischen Nachhaltigkeit von Software"), led by Prof. Dr. Verena Majuntke and funded by the Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Energy. Together with the Gesellschaft für Informatik (GI), we are a partner in the project, which works closely with DIN to develop a scientifically grounded standard for sustainable software — first as a DIN norm and later as an ISO standard. The goal is a comparable sustainability score covering energy as well as other resources, hardware and emissions.
